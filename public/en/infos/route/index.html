Route Saturday

Meeting point

10:00 Küblis

Schedule

The meeting point is at Küblis train station at 10:00. Once everyone has gathered, we will hike along the route to Klosters. There will be several short breaks and a lunch break along the way.

In Klosters, there will be dinner and an evening program.

The evening program will last until 11:00 p.m., and the shuttle to Fideris will leave at 8:00 p.m.!

The last train to Fideris will leave Klosters village at 11:27 p.m.. As there will probably not be enough seats on the bus, it would be great if people with a GA or GA Night travelcard could take the train. <3 (Tickets can also be refunded by us).

Route Sunday

Meeting points

10:00 a.m. Klosters or 10:45 Davos Larett for people joining on sunday!

Schedule

We will meet in Klosters, or if arriving later, in Davos Larett, and then travel together to Davos for the rally.

Return journey

There will be a special train for participants returning to Landquart at 6:06 p.m.. This is free of charge. The Soli-Busses will wait at Box-Davos, as they did last year. Important: Travel there in small groups (2-3 people)! Otherwise, the police will consider larger groups to be a gathering, which has caused problems in recent years.
